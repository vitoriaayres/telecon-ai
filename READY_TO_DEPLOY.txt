Issue: "Azure CLI not installed"
Fix:   Download from https://aka.ms/azure-cli

Issue: "Docker not running"
Fix:   Start Docker Desktop

Issue: "Script gets stuck"
Fix:   Wait 30 seconds (slow network), or Ctrl+C and re-run

Issue: "App returns 500 error"
Fix:   Check logs: az webapp log tail -g telecontrol-rg -n telecontrol-api

Issue: "Frontend can't reach backend (CORS error)"
Fix:   Restart backend: az webapp restart -g telecontrol-rg -n telecontrol-api

Issue: "Health endpoint returns 404"
Fix:   Wait 2-3 minutes for app to start, then retry
